Footprint 0.4.0

Keep the existing extension installation and folder, replace its contents with the new package, then click Reload in your browser's extensions page. Close old Footprint dashboards and reopen it.

New: full-sized post review cards, optional caption/image loading from original post pages, original links, Keep decisions, Needs review filtering, JSON inventory export, combined scanning of both Facebook tagged lists, incremental checkpoints, continuation from paused workers, and a friendlier shared visual palette.

A paused scan stays incomplete. Reaching a source's end does not prove Facebook exposed every historical tag. Missing/deleted/private posts may have no preview. No live-account removal or complete historical coverage is guaranteed.

Source: web-app/extension. See the root README and docs/0.4.0-validation.md.
